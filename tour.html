Waffles Atop the Seven Hills

Everyone knows Plovdiv for its age-old hills. But were you aware that every hill hides its own waffle superpower?

  • Sahat Tepe: Thanks to the clock tower, you get bonus minutes for dessert.
  • Bunardzhik: Towering Alyosha stands guard over every chocolate wafer.
  • Youth Hill (Mladezhki halm): Endless fuel for rolling around and munching.
